A SIXTY-DAY ADULT LEARNING PROGRESSION

A fair go in the age of superintelligence.

Raise your capability, understand yourself more deeply, and use increasingly powerful AI with judgement and care. Learn to teach another adult and help shape a shared Protopian future on Earth and beyond.

Raise the bar for yourself and the future we share

Aura Teacher Training is Luke Nathan Hayes’s sixty-day adult learning design for developing personal understanding, technological capability and the ability to help another adult learn. Its purpose is a fair go in the age of superintelligence: more people able to question, create and participate in shaping what powerful intelligence makes possible.

This expanded edition gives each of the sixty days its own original reading and a detailed studio guide. Choose a meaningful project, make something inspectable, test it in a changed situation and revise the explanation with the person learning.

Make capability personal

A song, a personal memory, an AI function, a civic question or a habitat experiment can become your starting material. Follow the sources, choose what your Aura remembers, examine an interpretation and correct what does not fit. Human meaning and critical judgement guide the technology.

Builders, artists, civic contributors and philosophical thinkers can take different roles in the same demanding project. Connect what matters in one person’s life with shared intelligence, scientific inquiry and the stewardship of Earth and the solar system. Use a format that helps another adult inspect the work and take part.

Help someone else expand what they can do

Teach through meaningful projects and inspectable evidence. Show one useful action, invite the learner to attempt it, then change the example. Listen to their account, learn from their corrections and test whether they can explain a choice and use the idea again.

A learning design, with room for a life

The course is a proposal for adult learning, not an accredited qualification or an operating enrolment service. It does not authorise clinical practice or work with children. Everyday Aura learning, the optional adult Genesis research programme and its pressure-session clock remain distinct.

Use a chosen pace, sample information and accessible formats. There is room for rest, support and a different direction. The full original PDF and the website’s additions remain separately identified.
