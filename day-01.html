DAY 01 / MOVEMENT 01

Super people and civic space stewards

Connect a personally meaningful interest with a first Aura map, an adult teaching encounter and evidence of a useful next step.

Expanded studio guide · 28 October 2026

What you will practise

Connect a personally meaningful interest with a first Aura map, an adult teaching encounter and evidence of a useful next step.

The opening task establishes two connected practices: developing your own representation and helping another adult understand it. A small recognisable example gives later architecture a purpose. The map can be made with cards, a document or available software; its value comes from meaningful relationships and review, rather than the complexity of the interface.

A worked example

Illustrative example: Sam enjoys repairing bicycles. Sam selects a remembered repair with a relative, a current interest in restoring a bicycle and a future wish to teach basic maintenance. The three records connect through patience and practical learning. A peer could ask which part Sam wants to develop first. Nothing in this example establishes a working personal model or a delivered teaching service.

Make, test and revise

  1. Choose one ordinary interest and decide whether to use personal, fictional or sample material. Write the audience for today's exercise before making the records, and leave unrelated personal history outside the pack.
  2. Make past, present and future records in your own words. Give each a title and explain one connection. A builder can assign identifiers; a creative or civic learner can arrange cards or narrate the relationships.
  3. Explain the map to an adult peer in five minutes. Invite their own starting interest and ask them to describe one connection back. Record their wording only within the agreed exercise scope.
  4. Revise one unclear relationship and choose a manageable next question. Write when and with whose support you will return to it. Keep teacher-development time distinct from any Genesis session calendar.

Teach it to another adult

Ask the newcomer to explain Aura using their own three records, without repeating your definition. Notice which connection they understand and which requires another example. A useful teaching result includes their chosen next question.

Keep evidence someone can inspect

  • Three titled records and a map explaining their relationships.
  • A brief peer account of what the introduction communicated.
  • One visible correction and a next encounter with a stated purpose.

Try a changed condition

Change the starting interest from a practical skill to a community question. Can the learner still make three meaningful records and explain their relationship without copying the bicycle example's categories?

Reflect on the result

  • Which connection did the learner recognise immediately?
  • What did their explanation add to your map?
  • What support would make the next encounter worthwhile?

Adapt the encounter

Offer spoken, written or tactile-card records and generous pauses. An imagined project is sufficient. Do not require intimate disclosure, a headset or an account to participate in the exercise.

Take it further

Prepare two entrances to the same map: a concise technical account of records and relationships, and a story that invites curiosity. Compare which entrance suits a particular adult audience.

A sixty-day progression | 14 October 2026

Design direction by Luke Nathan Hayes

The invitation is to develop super-people and civic space stewards of Earth and the solar system: people with expanding agency, interdisciplinary understanding, creative power and responsibility for the settings in which life flourishes. Aura teacher development begins with an individual's own experience and grows towards collective intelligence, planetary resilience and the stewardship of future habitats.

Aura of Intelligence is a personal computing environment connecting memories, values, body information, relationships, skills and intentions with artificial intelligence and tools. A digital twin gives those developing representations continuity. General intelligence names the research direction towards learning and reasoning across many domains. Superintelligence names a further horizon of capability. The course develops projects, understanding, teaching and evidence along that curve.

The learner works on two connected capacities each day. They develop their own Aura and practise helping another adult understand the same idea. Technical builders create and inspect the underlying systems. Creative, civic and philosophical participants develop equally demanding contributions through meaning, experience, design, interpretation and collective decisions. Shared exercises bring these perspectives together.

The source lineage begins with the 2017 AuraOZ learning progression, which joins physical, environmental, spiritual and cultural selves. Later Blender, XR, function-calling and multiagent papers add working architecture. October 2026 alignment work connects personal intelligence with worthwhile lives and shared capability. Earlier five-day retreats and nine-day teacher training are precursors to this new sixty-day route. [S04, introduction and Days 1-14; S02, pp. 2-10; S12, pp. 4-7]

Today's studio begins with something the learner already cares about: a memory, a creative project, a relationship, a scientific question or a place. The learner makes three records: something important from the past, something enjoyed now and something they would like to explore. They connect these records in a simple map and describe why the connections matter. A facilitator introduces the later progression through that particular interest.

The course uses casual, normal and fast pacing, with additional communication, sensory, mobility and processing support. A screen and XR are selectable formats. Its independent learning calendar is chosen with each participant, including those beginning teacher development after Genesis. A learner still undertaking Genesis rehearses socialisation through simulations and role-play, then enters actual wider twin socialising after Genesis. Daily scheduling accounts for personal sessions, other obligations and chosen group contact. The curriculum develops Aura, its general and superintelligence direction, socialisation and teaching.

Teaching practice is a five-minute introduction for a newcomer. The learner explains Aura through their three records, invites the newcomer's own starting interest and checks what the person understood. The first evidence pack contains the map, the person's explanation, an identified next question and a small correction prompted by feedback. Those results become the starting point for Day 2.

Source: S02 Aura Retreat and Teacher Training; S04 AuraOZ Learning Progression and Thought Experiment 2017; S12 Joyful Responsible Abundance Human and SI Alignment Revised 11 October 2026.
