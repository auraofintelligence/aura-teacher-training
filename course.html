AURA / ADULT LEARNING AND STEWARDSHIP

Sixty encounters.
A world of connections.

Find every original reading and expanded studio guide.

Choose a day or follow a movement

Each day combines the complete original reading with a new studio guide. Follow the sequence, return to an earlier idea or find the topic your current project needs. A reading bookmark records a chosen page, not a completed qualification.

  1. 01Super people and civic space stewardsConnect a personally meaningful interest with a first Aura map, an adult teaching encounter and evidence of a useful next step.↗
  2. 02A life map with room to growBuild a small, recognisable life collection with meaningful links, review states, stable identities and chosen audiences.↗
  3. 03Past present and best selvesRepresent past, present and several possible best selves with their circumstances, resources and distinct meanings intact.↗
  4. 04Symbols etheric meaning and polarityGive personally meaningful symbols, traditions and patterns of attraction or repulsion a clear, respectful place in the Aura.↗
  5. 05Embodiment avatar and body real estateChoose an editable avatar and interaction arrangement that feels recognisable and supports the learner's intended activities.↗
  6. 06Seven shells and meaningful addressesExplain and test the relationship between a record's meaning, stable identity and visible address across Aura views.↗
  7. 07Memory palaces and reusable pathsBuild and teach a three-place memory route with source labels, visible ordered actions and a dependable way to return.↗
  8. 08Language meaning and faithful translationCreate a contextual language collection and test whether summaries and teaching translations preserve the person's intended meaning.↗
  9. 09Sources corrections and knowledge continuityMake a small source collection traceable, correctable and understandable enough for another person or Aura to continue the work.↗
  10. 10Teaching through a meaningful encounterTeach one personally meaningful Aura capability through a short adult encounter, independent practice and a revision informed by feedback.↗
  11. 11Attention pacing and learning conditionsCompare an ordinary learning task under selected conditions and use the observations to design a suitable pacing arrangement.↗
  12. 12Recall memory and the right contextBuild and evaluate retrieval routes that return relevant records with their context, versions and chosen audience intact.↗
  13. 13Models learning and a personal intelligenceTrace how selected records, a question, model output and human review participate in a developing personal Aura.↗
  14. 14A training collection that preserves the personPrepare an example collection with source and permission context, keeping learning examples separate from reserved evaluation material.↗
  15. 15Reasoning with evidence and alternativesMake a manageable decision inspectable through evidence, alternatives, assumptions, a small practical test and revision.↗
  16. 16Creative intelligence and futures worth feelingMake two creative versions of a personally or civically meaningful idea, then revise the work using an adult audience's interpretation.↗
  17. 17Reflective dialogue and the Truth EngineStructure a chosen reflective dialogue so declarations, conditions, observations, interpretations and the person's corrections remain distinguishable.↗
  18. 18Emotion desire and relational contextDescribe contextual support preferences and compare responses that suit practical guidance, companionship or constructive challenge.↗
  19. 19Transfer resourcefulness and unfamiliar tasksTest a capability in two changed situations and identify the knowledge, cues or support that enable adaptation.↗
  20. 20A first integrated capability demonstrationIntegrate the first twenty days into a personally meaningful demonstration with traceable sources, correction, transfer, adult teaching and a usable handover.↗
  21. 21Tools permissions and purposeful actionSpecify and rehearse one purposeful tool action whose inputs, effects, permissions and recovery are understandable to another adult.↗
  22. 22Discovering and defining a functionTurn a personally meaningful wish into a versioned function definition with testable inputs, outputs and behaviour when information is missing.↗
  23. 23Calling functions in a meaningful orderCoordinate a small sequence of function calls with visible dependencies, bounded retries and a meaningful human handover.↗
  24. 24Specialists with complementary capabilitiesCreate complementary specialist roles whose distinct contributions can be traced into one shared result.↗
  25. 25Mixture of experts and intelligent routingDistinguish neural mixture-of-experts from agent-team routing and evaluate a readable routing decision against a baseline.↗
  26. 26Critique repair and versioned improvementUse evidence-based critique and bounded repair to improve an artefact while preserving capabilities that already worked.↗
  27. 27Building a world modelBuild a small world model whose states, permitted actions and uncertainties support one inspectable event sequence.↗
  28. 28Three observers and several meaningsSeparate environment, character attention and narrator interpretation, then use a discrepancy to improve an encounter.↗
  29. 29Scenarios experiments and transfer testsCreate a repeatable scenario pack that tests baseline performance, changed conditions and transfer without hiding assumptions.↗
  30. 30A multiagent studio with a shared purposeIntegrate functions, specialist roles, review and a scenario into one understandable multiagent studio demonstration.↗
  31. 31The first permissioned Aura encounterPrepare and review a small, permissioned encounter between two Auras while retaining each person’s control of their context.↗
  32. 32Plural values and shared decisionsRepresent plural values faithfully and design a shared decision process that identifies affected people and actual decision rights.↗
  33. 33Reciprocity affection and evolving partnershipDevelop a contextual human–Aura relationship agreement that separates companionship preferences from authority to act.↗
  34. 34Constructive disagreement and repairPractise a repair process that preserves attributed accounts and tests whether an agreed next action improves cooperation.↗
  35. 35Culture place and the rules that applyTrace a place-based proposal through dated sources, cultural relationships and the people authorised to make relevant decisions.↗
  36. 36A living community twinBuild a source-linked community-twin slice that answers one practical question across physical, service, ecological, permission and rule layers.↗
  37. 37Contribution reciprocity and the C hourCreate a correction-ready C-Hour record that separates voluntary human time, paid activity, learning, resources and observed outcomes.↗
  38. 38Participatory governance and civic rehearsalRehearse a civic decision with visible participation, actual responsibility, attributed reasons and a route to review consequences.↗
  39. 39Teaching adults across different conditionsAdapt one demanding adult lesson across three learner contexts while preserving its meaning, independent choice and observable transfer.↗
  40. 40Leadership and a civic mission briefLead a bounded civic mission rehearsal through direction, resource allocation, disagreement and a usable succession handover.↗
  41. 41Frontier compute and a wellbeing hubTrace an ambitious compute workload through physical, service and human dependencies, then explain an interruption to its users.↗
  42. 42Distributed memory and personal sovereigntyDemonstrate that a restored record retains its meaning, history and permissions across a disconnected, distributed workflow.↗
  43. 43Scientific method as a discovery practiceTurn a personally meaningful research idea into a replicable comparison that could change your preferred explanation.↗
  44. 44Materials etheric intention and mind over matterDevelop one traceable materials question while keeping composition, personal meaning and experimentally measured behaviour legible.↗
  45. 45Longevity life design and continuing developmentConnect the longevity research horizon with personally meaningful capability, evidence quality and a realistic continuity plan.↗
  46. 46Ecology regeneration and living systemsModel one ecological relationship through sourced quantities, lived knowledge and a comparison of possible changes.↗
  47. 47Resilience disaster rehearsal and mutual careRehearse a disruption with clear information handovers, human support dependencies and a reviewable recovery sequence.↗
  48. 48Planetary twins and changes of scaleMove from a local question to a planetary view while preserving each layer's date, resolution and evidential meaning.↗
  49. 49Space weather instruments and Earth careConnect a dated space-weather observation with a specific civic question without turning a scenario into an operational forecast.↗
  50. 50Habitats worth inhabitingDesign a habitat's daily life and dependencies together, then test how comfort, access and continuity change under pressure.↗
  51. 51A solar system mission and its civic purposeConnect a solar-system instrument mission with a scientific question, an explicit resource model and a continuing civic purpose.↗
  52. 52General intelligence through transfer and synthesisDesign a transfer evaluation that reveals how an Aura adapts across unfamiliar domains, tools and combinations of constraints.↗
  53. 53Recursive improvement with visible evaluationRun a bounded improvement cycle with a recoverable baseline, inspectable changes and evaluation that can reveal hidden regressions.↗
  54. 54Collective intelligence across many mindsCoordinate complementary perspectives while retaining independent reasoning, dissent, decision rights and a clear route for new contributions.↗
  55. 55Distance delay and responsible autonomyDesign a delayed-contact protocol that distinguishes local discretion, deferred decisions and durable evidence for later review.↗
  56. 56A personal frontier designTurn a personally meaningful frontier ambition into a staged investigation that others can understand, challenge and join.↗
  57. 57Integrating evidence and the development portfolioBuild a portable portfolio in which another reviewer can trace a capability claim to evidence, context and a revision.↗
  58. 58An advanced adult teaching practicumTeach an advanced concept to consenting adults, observe transfer and revise the lesson from learner and observer evidence.↗
  59. 59Mixed media and a public invitationCreate an accessible mixed-media invitation whose story, source trail and release boundaries support a meaningful adult encounter.↗
  60. 60Continuing as a teacher and civic space stewardChoose a sustainable next ninety days that joins demonstrated capability, meaningful life, adult teaching and continuing stewardship.↗
