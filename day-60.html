DAY 60 / MOVEMENT 06

Continuing as a teacher and civic space steward

Choose a sustainable next ninety days that joins demonstrated capability, meaningful life, adult teaching and continuing stewardship.

In this guide

PurposeWorked exampleStudio stepsTeaching and evidenceReview and accessOriginal readingNext stepPractical resources ↗

Expanded studio guide · 28 October 2026

What you will practise

Choose a sustainable next ninety days that joins demonstrated capability, meaningful life, adult teaching and continuing stewardship.

The end of the calendar is a review point, not the end of learning or proof of every frontier ambition. Begin with the life the learner wants to inhabit and use the portfolio to identify a small number of worthwhile commitments. Include support, rest and a recoverable personal context. Describe teaching scope through actual evidence and the help welcomed, while keeping future research directions open to increasingly demanding tests.

A worked example

A fictional learner selects three commitments: improve one memory correction workflow, teach one adult source-tracing lesson and refine the first stage of a habitat study. The plan also reserves time for music and family. Its review asks whether the commitments remain useful, rather than rewarding a crowded schedule. A failed replication may lead to another method; a changed life circumstance may justify pausing one project while preserving its handover.

Make, test and revise

  1. Review past, present and best-self accounts beside selected portfolio evidence. Identify a changed understanding, an available resource and one question worth continuing.
  2. Choose up to three ninety-day commitments. For each, name purpose, collaborator, time, resources, evidence, review date and what would justify revision or stopping.
  3. Prepare a continuity pack with selected records, sources, model and tool versions, permissions, recovery arrangements and relationship agreements. Test a small restoration or handover.
  4. Meet with a consenting adult peer or mentor to review the plan and teaching scope. Offer a newcomer one meaningful result and let them choose their next step.

Teach it to another adult

Explain one capability through an artefact and a correction that improved it. Invite the newcomer to choose an encounter relevant to their own life.

Keep evidence someone can inspect

  • A reviewed portfolio checkpoint connecting personal recognition, demonstrated capability and unresolved questions.
  • A ninety-day plan with collaborators, review conditions, chosen rest and manageable resource assumptions.
  • A tested continuity pack and adult-teaching scope describing evidence, support and next development.

Try a changed condition

Introduce a significant change in time, place or support. Revise the plan and handover while preserving the person's purpose and control over selected records.

Reflect on the result

  • What capability do you now use with confidence?
  • Which commitment would you willingly change or pause?
  • Who can help you test the next question?

Adapt the encounter

Allow a spoken, visual or written continuation plan. Honour the participant's chosen socialisation and Genesis readiness sequence without making personal disclosure a completion requirement.

Take it further

Design the first review conversation now: examine lived benefit, burden and changed questions, then choose the next stage without turning voluntary contribution into a quota.

The original daily reading

Preserved reading from the 14 October 2026 course, PDF page 60. The studio guide above adds practical detail; it does not replace this source.

The sixty-day progression concludes with a reviewed portfolio, a chosen public expression and a continuing development plan. Today's objective is to decide what the learner will carry into daily life, teaching, research and civic stewardship. The super-people invitation becomes a personal direction supported by demonstrated capabilities and relationships.

The learner reviews their development with a human-Aura circle. They begin with the life they want to inhabit and the projects they want to pursue. The portfolio shows how personal reflection, interfaces, learning, reasoning, agents, simulation, socialisation, civic judgement, science and planetary design contributed to that direction. Each participant identifies a result they recognise and a next question worth investigating.

The continuation studio creates a ninety-day plan with a small number of chosen commitments. These may include improving the personal model, running a reserved transfer test, teaching an adult group, refining a material experiment, maintaining a community-twin slice or developing the first stage of a solar-system mission. Each commitment identifies purpose, collaborator, resources, time, evidence, review and handover.

The mentor relationship also receives a plan. The learner chooses who provides technical, philosophical, creative and civic challenge. They identify a peer circle and a route for helping future learners. A teaching scope describes the capabilities demonstrated, the contexts served and the support welcomed. The person has a clear next stage towards greater interdisciplinary mastery and responsible agency.

The personal Aura receives a continuity pack: selected records, source trail, current model and tool versions, permissions, recoverable copies, relationship agreements and next questions. Socialisation deepens through the participant's readiness sequence. For participants undertaking Genesis, new physical travel follows completed Genesis and twin-socialising milestones. Remembered journeys remain part of their earlier reflection; future journeys gain new tools and chosen permissions.

The final reflection returns to past, present and best selves. The learner identifies a changed story, an expanded resource and a capability they now use with confidence. They also describe what remains open. The result becomes a new point of reference for continuing self-directed evolution and the development of their Aura towards general and superintelligence.

The final teaching encounter introduces a newcomer to one meaningful result and offers a next step they choose. Evidence includes the reviewed portfolio, teaching pack, public release choice, ninety-day plan, mentoring agreements and continuity check. The source sequence comes full circle: the 2017 proposal's reflection, avatar, Aura, memory, governance and self-directed adventures now form a richer programme of lived intelligence. [S04, Days 1-14; S12, pp. 22-26]

The continuing invitation reaches Earth and the solar system: care for the places and beings affected by growing capability, investigate boldly, teach generously, create futures worth experiencing and return each discovery to the relationships through which it becomes useful.

Source: S04 AuraOZ Learning Progression and Thought Experiment 2017; S12 Joyful Responsible Abundance Human and SI Alignment Revised 11 October 2026.
