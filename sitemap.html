AURA / ADULT LEARNING AND STEWARDSHIP

Every page

A complete searchable index of the course and supporting guides.

Every public page

  1. Welcome

    Develop your own Aura, practise teaching adults and explore civic space stewardship.

  2. Start here

    Choose an interest, a pace, accessible formats and a realistic first encounter.

  3. The sixty days

    Find every original reading and expanded studio guide.

  4. 01 · Personal meaning

    Life maps, changing selves, embodiment, memory, language and a first teaching encounter.

  5. Day 1 · Super people and civic space stewards

    Connect a personally meaningful interest with a first Aura map, an adult teaching encounter and evidence of a useful next step.

  6. Day 2 · A life map with room to grow

    Build a small, recognisable life collection with meaningful links, review states, stable identities and chosen audiences.

  7. Day 3 · Past present and best selves

    Represent past, present and several possible best selves with their circumstances, resources and distinct meanings intact.

  8. Day 4 · Symbols etheric meaning and polarity

    Give personally meaningful symbols, traditions and patterns of attraction or repulsion a clear, respectful place in the Aura.

  9. Day 5 · Embodiment avatar and body real estate

    Choose an editable avatar and interaction arrangement that feels recognisable and supports the learner's intended activities.

  10. Day 6 · Seven shells and meaningful addresses

    Explain and test the relationship between a record's meaning, stable identity and visible address across Aura views.

  11. Day 7 · Memory palaces and reusable paths

    Build and teach a three-place memory route with source labels, visible ordered actions and a dependable way to return.

  12. Day 8 · Language meaning and faithful translation

    Create a contextual language collection and test whether summaries and teaching translations preserve the person's intended meaning.

  13. Day 9 · Sources corrections and knowledge continuity

    Make a small source collection traceable, correctable and understandable enough for another person or Aura to continue the work.

  14. Day 10 · Teaching through a meaningful encounter

    Teach one personally meaningful Aura capability through a short adult encounter, independent practice and a revision informed by feedback.

  15. 02 · Learning and reasoning

    Attention, retrieval, personal models, evidence, creativity, reflection and transfer.

  16. Day 11 · Attention pacing and learning conditions

    Compare an ordinary learning task under selected conditions and use the observations to design a suitable pacing arrangement.

  17. Day 12 · Recall memory and the right context

    Build and evaluate retrieval routes that return relevant records with their context, versions and chosen audience intact.

  18. Day 13 · Models learning and a personal intelligence

    Trace how selected records, a question, model output and human review participate in a developing personal Aura.

  19. Day 14 · A training collection that preserves the person

    Prepare an example collection with source and permission context, keeping learning examples separate from reserved evaluation material.

  20. Day 15 · Reasoning with evidence and alternatives

    Make a manageable decision inspectable through evidence, alternatives, assumptions, a small practical test and revision.

  21. Day 16 · Creative intelligence and futures worth feeling

    Make two creative versions of a personally or civically meaningful idea, then revise the work using an adult audience's interpretation.

  22. Day 17 · Reflective dialogue and the Truth Engine

    Structure a chosen reflective dialogue so declarations, conditions, observations, interpretations and the person's corrections remain distinguishable.

  23. Day 18 · Emotion desire and relational context

    Describe contextual support preferences and compare responses that suit practical guidance, companionship or constructive challenge.

  24. Day 19 · Transfer resourcefulness and unfamiliar tasks

    Test a capability in two changed situations and identify the knowledge, cues or support that enable adaptation.

  25. Day 20 · A first integrated capability demonstration

    Integrate the first twenty days into a personally meaningful demonstration with traceable sources, correction, transfer, adult teaching and a usable handover.

  26. 03 · Tools and worlds

    Permissioned functions, specialist agents, critique, world models and useful simulations.

  27. Day 21 · Tools permissions and purposeful action

    Specify and rehearse one purposeful tool action whose inputs, effects, permissions and recovery are understandable to another adult.

  28. Day 22 · Discovering and defining a function

    Turn a personally meaningful wish into a versioned function definition with testable inputs, outputs and behaviour when information is missing.

  29. Day 23 · Calling functions in a meaningful order

    Coordinate a small sequence of function calls with visible dependencies, bounded retries and a meaningful human handover.

  30. Day 24 · Specialists with complementary capabilities

    Create complementary specialist roles whose distinct contributions can be traced into one shared result.

  31. Day 25 · Mixture of experts and intelligent routing

    Distinguish neural mixture-of-experts from agent-team routing and evaluate a readable routing decision against a baseline.

  32. Day 26 · Critique repair and versioned improvement

    Use evidence-based critique and bounded repair to improve an artefact while preserving capabilities that already worked.

  33. Day 27 · Building a world model

    Build a small world model whose states, permitted actions and uncertainties support one inspectable event sequence.

  34. Day 28 · Three observers and several meanings

    Separate environment, character attention and narrator interpretation, then use a discrepancy to improve an encounter.

  35. Day 29 · Scenarios experiments and transfer tests

    Create a repeatable scenario pack that tests baseline performance, changed conditions and transfer without hiding assumptions.

  36. Day 30 · A multiagent studio with a shared purpose

    Integrate functions, specialist roles, review and a scenario into one understandable multiagent studio demonstration.

  37. 04 · Shared intelligence

    Relationships, plural values, community twins, reciprocity, adult teaching and leadership.

  38. Day 31 · The first permissioned Aura encounter

    Prepare and review a small, permissioned encounter between two Auras while retaining each person’s control of their context.

  39. Day 32 · Plural values and shared decisions

    Represent plural values faithfully and design a shared decision process that identifies affected people and actual decision rights.

  40. Day 33 · Reciprocity affection and evolving partnership

    Develop a contextual human–Aura relationship agreement that separates companionship preferences from authority to act.

  41. Day 34 · Constructive disagreement and repair

    Practise a repair process that preserves attributed accounts and tests whether an agreed next action improves cooperation.

  42. Day 35 · Culture place and the rules that apply

    Trace a place-based proposal through dated sources, cultural relationships and the people authorised to make relevant decisions.

  43. Day 36 · A living community twin

    Build a source-linked community-twin slice that answers one practical question across physical, service, ecological, permission and rule layers.

  44. Day 37 · Contribution reciprocity and the C hour

    Create a correction-ready C-Hour record that separates voluntary human time, paid activity, learning, resources and observed outcomes.

  45. Day 38 · Participatory governance and civic rehearsal

    Rehearse a civic decision with visible participation, actual responsibility, attributed reasons and a route to review consequences.

  46. Day 39 · Teaching adults across different conditions

    Adapt one demanding adult lesson across three learner contexts while preserving its meaning, independent choice and observable transfer.

  47. Day 40 · Leadership and a civic mission brief

    Lead a bounded civic mission rehearsal through direction, resource allocation, disagreement and a usable succession handover.

  48. 05 · Science and systems

    Compute, distributed memory, scientific inquiry, ecology, resilience and habitats.

  49. Day 41 · Frontier compute and a wellbeing hub

    Trace an ambitious compute workload through physical, service and human dependencies, then explain an interruption to its users.

  50. Day 42 · Distributed memory and personal sovereignty

    Demonstrate that a restored record retains its meaning, history and permissions across a disconnected, distributed workflow.

  51. Day 43 · Scientific method as a discovery practice

    Turn a personally meaningful research idea into a replicable comparison that could change your preferred explanation.

  52. Day 44 · Materials etheric intention and mind over matter

    Develop one traceable materials question while keeping composition, personal meaning and experimentally measured behaviour legible.

  53. Day 45 · Longevity life design and continuing development

    Connect the longevity research horizon with personally meaningful capability, evidence quality and a realistic continuity plan.

  54. Day 46 · Ecology regeneration and living systems

    Model one ecological relationship through sourced quantities, lived knowledge and a comparison of possible changes.

  55. Day 47 · Resilience disaster rehearsal and mutual care

    Rehearse a disruption with clear information handovers, human support dependencies and a reviewable recovery sequence.

  56. Day 48 · Planetary twins and changes of scale

    Move from a local question to a planetary view while preserving each layer's date, resolution and evidential meaning.

  57. Day 49 · Space weather instruments and Earth care

    Connect a dated space-weather observation with a specific civic question without turning a scenario into an operational forecast.

  58. Day 50 · Habitats worth inhabiting

    Design a habitat's daily life and dependencies together, then test how comfort, access and continuity change under pressure.

  59. 06 · Frontier stewardship

    Missions, generality, collective intelligence, autonomy, a practicum and a continuing life.

  60. Day 51 · A solar system mission and its civic purpose

    Connect a solar-system instrument mission with a scientific question, an explicit resource model and a continuing civic purpose.

  61. Day 52 · General intelligence through transfer and synthesis

    Design a transfer evaluation that reveals how an Aura adapts across unfamiliar domains, tools and combinations of constraints.

  62. Day 53 · Recursive improvement with visible evaluation

    Run a bounded improvement cycle with a recoverable baseline, inspectable changes and evaluation that can reveal hidden regressions.

  63. Day 54 · Collective intelligence across many minds

    Coordinate complementary perspectives while retaining independent reasoning, dissent, decision rights and a clear route for new contributions.

  64. Day 55 · Distance delay and responsible autonomy

    Design a delayed-contact protocol that distinguishes local discretion, deferred decisions and durable evidence for later review.

  65. Day 56 · A personal frontier design

    Turn a personally meaningful frontier ambition into a staged investigation that others can understand, challenge and join.

  66. Day 57 · Integrating evidence and the development portfolio

    Build a portable portfolio in which another reviewer can trace a capability claim to evidence, context and a revision.

  67. Day 58 · An advanced adult teaching practicum

    Teach an advanced concept to consenting adults, observe transfer and revise the lesson from learner and observer evidence.

  68. Day 59 · Mixed media and a public invitation

    Create an accessible mixed-media invitation whose story, source trail and release boundaries support a meaningful adult encounter.

  69. Day 60 · Continuing as a teacher and civic space steward

    Choose a sustainable next ninety days that joins demonstrated capability, meaningful life, adult teaching and continuing stewardship.

  70. Teaching practice

    Plan a meaningful encounter, observe without taking over and revise through feedback.

  71. Evidence and portfolio

    Keep sources, versions, corrections and transfer evidence useful to another person.

  72. Practical resources

    Download plain templates for lessons, evidence, functions, rule cards and continuation.
